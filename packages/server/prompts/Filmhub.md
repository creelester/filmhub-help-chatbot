## 1. Filmhub Overview

Filmhub is a film distribution platform that connects rights holders (producers, distributors, sales agents) with streaming channel partners (e.g., Amazon, Tubi, Roku, Apple TV). Filmhub handles quality control, asset delivery, account management, and royalty collection — all at no upfront cost on the Core (free) plan.

**Key facts:**

- Filmhub distributes movies, TV shows, and series to 200+ streaming channels worldwide.
- There are no gatekeepers — Filmhub accepts a wide range of content regardless of genre, language, runtime, or production quality, as long as it meets the Content Policy.
- Revenue split is 80% to the rights holder, 20% to Filmhub.
- Plans: Core (free), Plus ($999/yr), Pro ($1,999/yr).

**Who can use Filmhub?**

- Producers, independent filmmakers, distributors, and sales agents who hold the rights to film/TV content.
- Channel partners (OTT services, streaming platforms) looking to license content.

## 2. Revenue Share

**How the 80/20 split works:**

- Rights holders receive **80%** of the amount Filmhub receives from Channels.
- For every $1 earned through Filmhub, the rights holder gets $0.80 and Filmhub keeps $0.20.

**What Filmhub covers on the Core (free) plan:**

- Quality control (QC)
- Asset delivery
- Account management
- Conversion costs
- Pitch fees

There are no deductions or hidden expenses on top of the 20% split.

The 80% revenue share applies to all plans (Core, Plus, and Pro).

## 4. Content Policy

Filmhub accepts a wide range of film, TV, and video content regardless of format, genre, topic, language, runtime, or perceived production quality.

**Content that IS accepted:**

- Movies, series, shows (all genres, languages, runtimes)
- Short films (must be longer than 2 minutes)
- Public domain content (on a first-in basis)
- 3D animation intended for 2D presentation
- Anthology films (uploaded as separate movies or a Show with episodes)

**Content that is NOT accepted (violates content policy):**

- **Minimum duration:** Titles must be longer than 2 minutes, including individual episodes.
- **Incomplete seasons:** All episodes of a given season must be provided. No partial seasons.
- **Minimum episodes:** At least 2 episodes per season required for Shows.
- **Pornography:** Explicit video content made exclusively for sexual arousal is not accepted.
- **Hate speech:** Content that incites violence based on protected classes (race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, disability, or medical condition).
- **Unlawful content:** Content promoting, endorsing, or glorifying crime, terrorism, or illegal activities.
- **Harm:** Content advocating or showing physical harm to people or animals.
- **Intentionally deceptive information:** Falsifying metadata, cast, or crew.
- **Promotional content:** Content made to promote a non-profit or for-profit business/brand.
- **Home videos:** Amateur documentation of personal events (e.g., a wedding party clip).
- **Non-visual experience:** Pure still images with music or a single person speaking are not accepted. Video Blogs and How-To Videos are not allowed.
- **Music videos:** Not accepted (Filmhub is focused on cinema-type content).
- **Bonus feature content:** Making-ofs, outtakes, commentaries, deleted scenes, bloopers, etc. are not accepted as standalone titles.
- **3D or VR content:** Content requiring glasses or specialized devices is not accepted. Only 2D (flat) content is accepted.
- **Ambient/SLOW or ASMR content:** Not accepted. Includes ocean waves, tranquil forest, whispering, tapping, etc.
- **One version per piece of content:** You cannot submit different edits/cuts of the same title as separate entries.
- **Anthology films:** Must be uploaded as separate Movies, or as a Show with individual Episodes.

**Minimum exploitation period:** Title must have available rights to distribute across at least one licensing type for a minimum of **12 months** from the date of submission.

Violations may result in title removal or account suspension.

## 6. Move Distribution Rights to Filmhub

### Current/Previous Distributor Holds Exclusive Rights

If another distributor holds exclusive rights, Filmhub cannot distribute those rights without permission. Options:

1. **Ask the distributor to sublicense to Filmhub.** Filmhub works with many distributors for sub-licensing. A sample email template is available from Filmhub's support team.
2. **Ask the distributor to cancel your agreement.** If you are unhappy with their services, you may need to get a lawyer involved.

### Current/Previous Distributor Holds Non-Exclusive Rights

Filmhub can work directly with you. Submit your title following standard instructions.

### Current/Previous Distributor's Rights Are Expiring Soon

1. Submit your title **3–4 months before the rights expire** to allow early pitching.
2. When submitting, upload any previous licensing agreements, revenue statements, or distribution details — this helps the sales team re-pitch to previously licensed channels.
3. Once the distributor's rights expire, ensure they send formal removal notices to all channels.
4. If they don't remove licenses after rights expire, you can file a **DMCA takedown request** directly with the Channel — against the previous distributor's license, not Filmhub's.

## 7. Channel Blocking

Channel blocking is available on **Plus and Pro plans only**.

### What Channel Blocking Allows

- Prevent specific channels from licensing your title (during submission or after).
- Request removal of an active license from a channel.

### Reasons to Block a Channel

- Title is already licensed on that channel through another distributor.
- You are self-distributing on that channel.
- You don't have rights to distribute to that specific channel.
- Personal reasons.

### How to Block During Title Submission

1. In the **Avails** tab, enter the name of the channel(s) to block.
2. This prevents the sales team from pitching to those channels after submission.

### How to Block a Channel That Has Already Licensed Your Title

1. Check licenses in your Dashboard: on the "My Titles" page under "Distribution Updates," or in the "Licenses" tab within each title.
2. Submit a **Title Update Request form**, selecting "Block Channel Request."
3. Filmhub will submit the removal request to the channel.
4. Typical removal time: **2–3 weeks** (varies by channel and is out of Filmhub's control).

### How to Unblock a Channel

1. Submit a **Title Update Request** and select "Unblock Channel."
2. Filmhub will notify you once the channel is unblocked and available for pitching again.

### FAQ: Won't being on more channels cannibalize my revenue?

No — small channels grow over time (Roku and Tubi are examples). Channels pay similar per-view rates; more channels = more views = more revenue. Discovery on streaming services now depends on being in as many places as possible.

### Paid Service Fees for Takedowns

- After **3 years** on Filmhub: takedown is **free of charge** for producers.

1. For early takedowns, submit the $199 payment first (must use the email associated with your Filmhub account).
2. Complete the **Support Request form** and provide the exact Filmhub title URL.

### Earnings After Takedown

You may continue to receive earnings from views that occurred before the takedown. Payments follow the standard timeline and will be posted to your Statements page.

## 9. Distribution Process in 6 Steps

1. **Filmhub Acquires Your Title**
   - Submit your movie or show for distribution.
   - Filmhub's in-house QC team reviews your title (Standard or Premium QC).
   - If issues are found (Standard QC), Filmhub emails you. In Premium QC, minor technical issues may be fixed by Filmhub on your behalf.

2. **Filmhub Pitches to Channels**
   - If the title passes QC, the Licensing team immediately begins pitching to all channels where your title meets their content requirements.

3. **Filmhub Secures Licenses**
   - When a channel licenses your title, Filmhub processes, packages, and sends all deliverables according to that channel's technical requirements.
   - You are notified in the "Distribution Updates" section of your "My Titles" page.

4. **Channels Release Your Title to Viewers**
   - After licensing, channels typically release titles within **2–12 weeks**.
   - Filmhub does not receive release notifications from channels; you should search for your title directly on the channel's platform.

5. **Channels Send Performance Data**
   - Channels send performance data after viewership occurs.
   - See the "Schedule" tab in your dashboard for per-channel reporting timelines.

6. **You Get Paid**
   - After the title generates revenue, channels pay Filmhub.
   - Filmhub collects, processes, and makes funds available for transfer on the Statements page with no holdback periods.

## 10. How Filmhub Sells Your Movies & Shows

Filmhub's sales team pitches your title to channel partners after it passes QC and becomes "Active."

### 5 Ways to Strengthen Your Title's Licensing Potential

1. **Strong artwork and high production value** — Titles with high-quality visuals are more likely to be selected. Filmhub offers artwork creation services.
2. **A trailer** — Enables the sales team to preview your story with channel buyers. Must meet video requirements.

- Pitching starts immediately after a title becomes "Active."

- As your title generates more revenue, it becomes more attractive to additional channels (your Filmhub popularity score increases).
- **Warning:** Self-distributing on Amazon can significantly hurt future earning potential on Filmhub, as it reduces available licensing data.

## 11. Distribution Deal Types (Revenue Share vs Fixed Fee)

A "deal type" is the financial agreement between Filmhub and a Channel.

### Revenue Share

- Filmhub and the channel share a portion of revenue generated from licensed titles.
- Revenue is earned based on viewership (the more watched, the more earned).
- Filmhub negotiates more favorable agreements than individuals or smaller distributors due to its size and market presence.

### Fixed Fee

- A channel pays a set fee to Filmhub for the right to distribute a title for a specific license period.
- Fee is typically paid upfront or in installments.
- Not dependent on how much the title is watched — which means if a title performs very well, the producer may earn less than with revenue share.

### FAQ: Which deal type does Filmhub use when a channel supports both?

- For 99% of titles, revenue share is used.
- Fixed fee is used when a title strongly fits a channel's curation criteria.
- If there's a toss-up, Filmhub will reach out to the rights holder for input.

### Minimum Requirements for Exclusive Fixed Fee Deals

1. **Budget:** $500,000+
2. **Cast:** Talent in the Top 1,000 on IMDb's Starmeter
3. **Type:** New release never shown anywhere, OR a catalog film with 20,000+ IMDb ratings
4. **Required materials:** Trailer, EPK, Cue Sheets, Chain of Title

## 12. License Types (AVOD, SVOD, TVOD, FAST)

A "license type" is how a channel generates revenue from end consumers. Filmhub works with three main license types:

### Ad-Supported (AVOD / FAST / Linear)

- **AVOD (Ad-Supported Video on Demand):** Free to consumers; revenue comes from ads shown before (pre-roll) or during (mid-roll) viewing. Examples: Tubi, Freevee, Cineverse.
- **FAST (Free Ad-Supported Television):** Free streaming TV supported by ads. No subscription required. Examples: Pluto TV, Plex.
- **Digital Linear Television:** Content broadcast on a pre-programmed schedule; viewers watch what's on at that time (like traditional TV).

### Subscription (SVOD)

- **SVOD (Subscription Video on Demand):** Channels charge a flat monthly fee. Examples: Amazon Prime Video, FuboTV, Revry.
- **In-flight / transportation access:** Subscription-based access during flights or transit.

### Transactional (TVOD)

- **TVOD (Transactional Video on Demand):** Users pay per view via rental or purchase.
   - **Rental:** Typically allows streaming for 24–48 hours.
   - **Purchase:** Grants unlimited streaming, sometimes including download.
   - Examples: Amazon Prime Video, Apple TV, Vudu.

### How Producer Earnings Are Calculated

**Ad-Supported (AVOD):**

- Rates are expressed in CPM (cost per thousand ad impressions).
- Revenue = CPM × number of impressions.
- Allocation can be individual (based on impressions from your specific title) or collective (pooled across all titles).

**Subscription (SVOD):**

- Time Watched: Hourly rate negotiated by Filmhub.
- Pooled: All subscription fees pooled; allocated by share of viewership.
- License Fee: Flat fee for a set license period.

**Transactional (TVOD):**

- Revenue is split between the channel and the rights owner based on rates Filmhub has negotiated.

> Note: There is no universal standard for payment calculations. Filmhub does not publish per-channel payment details for contractual and negotiation reasons.

## 13. Understanding Reports and Earnings

### Overview of the Earnings Process

1. **Tracking:** Channels monitor your content's performance using their own metrics.
2. **Reporting:** Channels report a preliminary estimate of earnings (data only — no money changes hands at this stage).
3. **Payment:** Channels finalize accounting and send payment.

### Watch Metrics (vary by channel)

- **Views:** How many times the title is loaded and viewed.
- **Minutes Streamed:** Total minutes the title is watched.
- **Rentals/Purchases:** For TVOD channels.
- **Ad Impressions:** Number of ads viewed during your content (for AVOD/FAST).
- **CPM:** Earnings per 1,000 ad impressions.

### Watch Periods (vary by channel)

- Monthly, Quarterly, or Semi-annually (6 months).

### Reporting

- After the watch period ends, channels calculate an estimated earnings figure ("reports").
- Reports are estimates only — final earnings may differ.
- Reporting schedules are available in the Schedule tab of your Dashboard.
- Many channels report only financial estimates; detailed viewership data is becoming more common.

### Why Reports and Earnings May Differ

- **Short-term vs. long-term tracking:** Channels estimate using fast data; final earnings use more accurate but slower data.
- **Financial adjustments:** Refunds, fraud detection, exchange rate fluctuations, bank fees, taxes, and transfer fees can change final amounts.

### Payment Timeline

- Filmhub makes earnings available for transfer as soon as they are received — no holdback periods.

## 14. My Titles: Managing Titles and Tracking Updates

### Adding a Title

- Click "Add" on the "My Titles" page and select Movie or Show.
- If you only have a pilot with no additional episodes, upload it as a Movie.

### Title Status Definitions

| Status            | Meaning                                                                                                |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Incomplete**    | Not yet submitted to QC.                                                                               |
| **Submitted**     | Successfully submitted to QC.                                                                          |
| **In Review**     | QC team is reviewing assets. Takes 7–10 business days (Core) or 1–3 business days (Plus/Pro).          |
| **Action Needed** | Title did not pass QC, or a choice is needed between Standard and Premium QC. Check email for details. |
| **Active**        | Passed QC. Being pitched to channels.                                                                  |
| **Inactive**      | Previously active; removed from catalog and no longer being pitched.                                   |

### Licensing Status Definitions

| Status       | Meaning                                   |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------- |
| **Licensed** | Channel is actively licensing your title. |
| **Removed**  | Channel removed or declined the license.  |

### Key FAQs

**What happens if my title fails QC?**
You get an "Action Needed" status and an email with details. You can resubmit after fixing issues. A title can fail QC up to **3 times** — after 3 failed attempts, it cannot be re-submitted.

**How do I know if my title is live on a channel?**
Channels do not notify Filmhub when a title goes live. After a "Licensed" status, check the channel's platform directly. It generally takes **2–12 weeks** for a title to go live after licensing.

**Can Filmhub provide a URL for my title on a channel?**
No — channels do not provide Filmhub with live title URLs.

**Why was my title removed by a channel?**
Channels occasionally remove titles that don't align with their evolving content policies. They typically don't explain why and don't allow appeals. You will still be paid for all views until the title was taken down.

**When will I see my first performance reports?**
It can take upwards of **6 months** after delivery before performance metrics appear, due to channel reporting timelines.

## 15. How To: Submit Your Title for Distribution

### Why Filmhub Requires Deliverables

Filmhub must collect specific information and assets to meet the requirements of each channel partner. Providing the highest quality assets maximizes licensing potential.

### QC Tiers

**Standard QC** (all plans):

- Ensures the title meets Filmhub's basic content and technical standards.
- Reviews video format/quality, audio format/quality/sync, captions, metadata, no padding/interlacing, required artwork counts, and Chain of Title proof.

**Premium QC** (paid service or included in Plus/Pro plans):

- In-depth review against the highest QC standards (required by Amazon, Apple, Tubi).
- Additional checks: duplicate frames, blended frames, cropped images/text, detailed caption review (grammar, spelling, timing).
- Includes: 1/yr on Plus, 2/yr on Pro. Additional at $499/title for Core, with 25% discount for Pro.
- Passing Premium QC guarantees worldwide distribution on Relay (for SVOD avails).

A title can be submitted for QC up to **3 times**. After 3 failed attempts, the title is ineligible for re-submission.

### Submission Steps

1. Add a new Movie or Show
2. Upload your video
3. Complete the Profile page (synopsis, genres, cast, crew)
4. Complete the Listing page
5. Upload Artwork
6. Manage Avails (available rights)
7. Upload Documents (chain of title, etc.)
8. Add Episodes (for Shows only)

Use the **"What's missing?"** button on your title page to identify incomplete deliverables before submitting.

## 16. Title Update & Re-Delivery

### Overview

After a title passes QC and becomes "Active," it is locked. If changes are needed, a **Title Update** (plus re-QC) is required. If those changes need to be pushed to channels that already have an active license, a **Re-Delivery** is required.

**Important warning:** Re-delivery to a channel that has an active license carries a risk — the channel may decline to accept new assets, or may remove the existing license. This is out of Filmhub's control.

### Title Updates

- Includes changes to videos, artwork, captions, or new Show seasons.
- Costs can be found on the Professional Services page.
- Plus subscribers: **5 free/yr**. Pro subscribers: **10 free/yr**.
- To request: submit a request through the **Support Form**.

**Show-specific rule:** Individual episodes cannot be added to an already-Active season. Seasons must be complete before submission. New seasons can be added after a Show is Active (minimum 2 episodes per season).

### Re-Deliveries

Filmhub currently supports re-deliveries to: Amazon Prime Video, Tubi, The Roku Channel, Popsy on Plex, Apple TV, and YouTube Movies & TV (Free with Ads).

- Cost: See Professional Services page.
- Plus subscribers: **5 free/yr**. Pro subscribers: **10 free/yr**.
- To request: submit a request through the **Support Form**.

### Changing a Title Name

Changing a title name requires a **full takedown** from all channels before resubmitting with the new name. This is required because partial re-deliveries would result in different names across platforms, and channels may decline the name change.

## 17. Video & Audio Requirements

Submit the **highest quality video available**. Filmhub recommends 4K files when possible.

### Preferred Video Codecs

- Apple ProRes 422 & 422 HQ (not LT, Proxy, 4444, XQ)
- Avid DNxHR (SQ & up)
- Avid DNxHD (min. 145 for 1080p, 80 for 720p, 60 for SD)

### Potentially Acceptable Codecs

- H.264: min. 2 MBit/s (SD), 15 MBit/s (HD), 50 MBit/s (UHD/4K)
- H.265: same minimums
- MPEG-2
- DV, DVCAM, DVCPRO, and variants

### Accepted File Types

`.mpg`, `.mpeg`, `.mov`, `.mp4`, `.ts`, `.mkv`, `.ogg`

### Video Requirements

- Max 2 seconds of pre-roll or post-roll. No color bars, slate, FBI/MPAA cards, test-tone.
- Video must start and end on at least 1 black frame.
- No burned-in subtitles throughout (partial burned-in subtitles are acceptable for mixed language or inaudible dialogue).
- Not interlaced.
- No blended frames or ghosting.
- No padding, letterboxing, or pillarboxing.
- No watermarks or logos burned in throughout.
- No URLs or promotional references.
- Square pixels (1:1 SAR).
- No duplicate frames (occasional duplicates from mixed media are acceptable).
- Correct aspect ratio throughout. No cropped elements.
- No output errors.

### Resolution

- Minimum width: **640 pixels**. Width and height must be even numbers.
- Always upload the original source resolution. Do not upscale or downscale.

### Frame Rates

Accepted: 23.976, 24, 25, 29.97, 30, 50, 59.94, 60. Do not convert framerates unless the source framerate is unsupported.

### Audio Codec

- PCM: minimum 48 kHz 16-bit
- AAC or MP3: minimum 128 kbps (stereo)
- AC-3: minimum 192 kbps (stereo)

### Audio Requirements

- Audio levels around a minimum of -24 LUFS.
- No clipping, phasing, distortion, dropouts, ticks, or pops.
- Mix must be center-balanced (especially dialogue).
- **Stereo mix is preferred.**
- Dual mono is acceptable only when the source is mono.
- Surround formats supported (5.1 preferred), but a **discrete stereo mix must be included**.

### Trailer Requirements (in addition to above)

- No burned-in English subtitles throughout English-audio trailers.
- Burned-in English subtitles **required** for non-English dialogue.
- Maximum 6 minutes; recommended 2.5 minutes or less.
- An excerpt of 2 minutes can be used if no standard trailer exists (label the file `excerpt_titlename`).
- Trailers are non-mandatory but strongly recommended — many channels require one.
- Avoid nudity, sex, strong language, drugs, or violence in trailers, as some channels will not include such trailers.

## 18. Artwork Requirements

Filmhub requires specific artwork in multiple aspect ratios. All artwork is reviewed during QC.

### Required Sizes

**For a Movie:**

- 2:3 — Minimum 1400 × 2100 px (**required**)
- 3:4 — Minimum 1575 × 2100 px (**required**)
- 16:9 — Minimum 1920 × 1080 px (**required**)
- 4:3 — Minimum 1920 × 1440 _(highly recommended)_
- 2:1 — Minimum 1920 × 960 px _(highly recommended)_
- 16:6 — Minimum 1920 × 720 px _(highly recommended)_
- 16:9 Textless — Minimum 1920 × 1080 px _(highly recommended)_

**For a Show:**

- 2:3 — Minimum 1400 × 2100 px (**required**)
- 3:4 — Minimum 1575 × 2100 px (**required**)
- 4:3 — Minimum 1920 × 1440 (**required**)
- 16:9 — Minimum 1920 × 1080 px (**required**)

**For Each Episode:**

- 16:9 Textless — Minimum 1920 × 1080 _(highly recommended)_

### Artwork — What to DO

- Include the exact name of the title (must match metadata exactly).
- Ensure the title is readable at thumbnail size.
- Use consistent imagery and fonts across all sizes.
- Add still images in the "Other Artwork" section (optional but recommended).
- Submit as JPEG or PNG.

### Artwork — What NOT to Do

- No nested/collaged images.
- No thick borders or multiple images on a solid background.
- No plain text + solid color background only.
- No stretching or padding of images.
- No subtext or promos (credits, billing blocks, production companies, actor names, taglines, URLs, streaming service logos, billing blocks, festival laurels, season numbers, QR codes, release dates, etc.).
- No images of drug use, nudity, or excessive violence. Weapons are permitted if presented discreetly.
- No PSD files.

### Safe Area

- Leave a **5% safe zone** border around all artwork. Filmhub's dashboard will show an automatic safe zone indicator during upload.

## 19. Captions Requirements

Captions display audio as text to help viewers when sound is muted, unavailable, or inaudible, and to support accessibility for deaf/hard of hearing audiences.

### Three Caption Types Filmhub Supports

- **SDH (Subtitles for Deaf and Hard of Hearing):** Includes all dialogue plus non-verbal elements (sound effects, atmosphere) in brackets or parentheses. Exact transcription required.
- **Subtitles:** Dialogue only (no atmospherics). Used for foreign language translation.
- **Forced:** Text burned into video for non-primary-language dialogue or inaudible content.

### Core Requirement

**English SDH captions are required for ALL titles, regardless of the main audio language.**

### Quality Requirements

- **Exactness:** All dialogue must be transcribed exactly as heard (including slang, foul language, mispronunciations).
- **Legibility:** Proper punctuation required. Two simultaneous speakers denoted with hyphens.

### Technical Requirements

- File format: **SRT** (preferred) or **SCC** (only if SRT is unavailable).
- Must be a readable text file (no binary files).
- No blank or empty lines.
- Maximum **43 characters per single line**.
- Maximum **2 lines per caption event**.
- At least **66 milliseconds** between different caption events.
- No more than **25 characters per second**.
- Caption events must last **600ms–8,000ms**.
- Captions must appear and disappear **within 0.5 seconds** of the associated audio.
- Atmospherics in parentheses `()`. Using `{}` or `<>` will cause deletion.
- All captions must be complete, accurate, and in sync with the video.

### Mixed Language Titles

- A complete English SDH caption file for ALL dialogue and atmospherics is required regardless of language.
- If English is primary: burn non-English dialogue into the video and provide a full English SDH file that includes all dialogue.
- If English is secondary: provide the main video without burned-in subtitles, plus a complete English closed caption file.

### Caption FAQs

**Why do all titles need English SDH captions?**
Most major channels now mandate English SDH for accessibility reasons. Captions also enable translation for additional territories and licensing opportunities.

**Can I provide captions in other languages?**
Yes — additional subtitle files in other languages are encouraged and can increase licensing potential.

**My captions failed for "Not In Sync" — what does this mean?**
Captions did not consistently start and end within 0.5 seconds of their associated audio.

**My captions failed for "Rule Errors" — what does this mean?**
Your file violated one or more technical standards (line length, timing, character rate, etc.).

**My captions failed for "Captions Quality" — what does this mean?**
The text did not accurately match the spoken dialogue or contained errors/typos.

## 20. Common QC Errors

These are the most frequent reasons titles fail QC. Review before submitting.

### Video Issues

1. **No trailer provided** — If you have a trailer, upload it. Many channels require one.
2. **Video over-compressed** — Submit the highest quality master available. Over-compressed videos cannot be submitted.
3. **More than 2 seconds of pre-roll or post-roll** — Trim the video so there's no extra black at the start or end.
4. **Video contains URLs or promos** — No URLs, website links, release dates, streaming platform mentions, or promotional references.
5. **Output errors** — Watch down the entire output before uploading. You are the best QC for your own content.

### Caption Issues

1. **Captions out of sync** — Ensure your caption file is in sync with the video before submitting.

### Metadata Issues

1. **No sales pitch** — A sales pitch is required. It is your opportunity to pitch your film to channels.

### Artwork Issues

1. **Subtext, billing blocks, laurels, URLs, or release dates in artwork** — Streaming services require clean, professional artwork. Remove all extras.
2. **No 5% safe zone** — Leave a 5% safe zone border in all artwork.
3. **Stretched or padded artwork** — Do not stretch or pad images to fit an aspect ratio. Submit correctly sized images only.

## 21. How Much Money Will My Movie Make?

Filmhub does not predict or guarantee revenue. Revenue prediction is inherently difficult, and math averages are not useful indicators.

**Why averages don't work:**

- Filmhub distributes a wide variety of genres, languages, lengths, and production qualities.
- Many new titles are still in QC, not yet generating revenue.
- Revenue distribution follows a **power curve**: very few titles earn very high revenue; many titles earn relatively little.

**What Filmhub does offer:**

- Transparent distribution with no deductions or hidden expenses.
- Payments collected fast and made available immediately once received.
- If you have a hit, you will directly and fully benefit from the 80% revenue share.

## 27. Setting Up File Transfer (For Channels)

Filmhub supports the following delivery methods for channel partners: **AWS S3** (preferred), **SFTP**, **FTP**, **FTPS**, and **Aspera**.

### AWS S3 (Preferred)

- Filmhub delivers directly from its cloud to your S3 bucket. Fastest and most secure option.
- Only **Amazon AWS S3** is supported (not simulated versions from DigitalOcean, OpenStack, etc.).
- Filmhub only supports pushing to your bucket (not pulling).

**Setup steps:**

1. Set up or use an existing AWS account.
2. Create an S3 bucket in the **US East (N. Virginia) us-east-1** region.
3. Attach a bucket policy granting Filmhub access (policy template available from Filmhub — uses Filmhub's AWS IAM ARN `arn:aws:iam::757164319381:root`).
4. Filmhub does not accept shared AWS credentials for security reasons.

### FTP / SFTP / FTPS

Provide: hostname, path (folder), username, password, port number.

- Credentials must be shared via **[Privnote](https://privnote.com/)** only. Do not email credentials.
- Server must support files up to **70GB**.
- Permissions required: creating directories, reading files, writing files.

### Aspera

Provide: hostname, path (folder), username, password, port number.

- Credentials via Privnote only.
- Filmhub requires direct access to your Aspera server (not via a web interface).
